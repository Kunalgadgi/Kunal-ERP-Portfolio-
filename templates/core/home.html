Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about my freelance services.

Do you provide ERPNext support in India?

Yes, I offer dedicated ERPNext support and implementation services for manufacturing, retail, and service businesses across India, including Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Delhi, and other major cities.

Can you build custom apps for Frappe?

Absolutely. I specialize in developing Custom Frappe Apps to extend the functionality of ERPNext or create standalone business applications tailored to your specific workflows.

How do you handle data migration to ERPNext?

I provide comprehensive data migration services, ensuring a smooth transition from legacy systems like Tally, Excel, or other ERPs. I handle data cleaning, validation, and mapping to ensure zero data loss during the switch to Frappe/ERPNext.

Do you offer post-deployment support?

Yes, I offer flexible Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and on-demand support packages. This includes server monitoring, security updates, bug fixes, and minor feature enhancements to keep your business running without interruption.
